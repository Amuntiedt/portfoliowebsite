Maui Wildfire Analysis

More stuff about the Maui Wildfire map. I did it on GEE. I conducted a series of analyses on the 2023 Maui Wildfire using a Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR), Normalized Difference Mouisture Index (MDMI), and a Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) looking at both before and after the wildfire.
