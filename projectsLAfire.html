LA Wildfire Analysis

More stuff about the LA wildfire map. I did it on GEE. Same as Hawaii but this time I considered wind patterns. I made a series of maps of the LA Wildfires looking at Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) conditions leading up to the fire as well as total area burnt. I also analyzed past climate trends as well as ateh impact of the Santa Ana winds in the area.
